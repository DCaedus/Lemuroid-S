Lemuroid-S is a free, open-source retro game emulator built on Lemuroid and libretro. It keeps Lemuroid's simplicity and adds features modeled on RetroArch: one folder for all your games, saves and covers, RetroArch-compatible saves, up-to-date emulator cores and more systems. It's light on battery and runs well on older phones. No ads, no tracking, no "All files access".

Features:
- One Lemuroid-S folder for ROMs (optional), BIOS files, saves, save states and covers, sorted by system. Your saves stay in your storage, survive reinstalls and are easy to back up
- Games load straight from your folders, zipped or not, without copying them first
- RetroArch-compatible saves, and Import Saves from RetroArch, other emulators or a ZIP file
- Covers like RetroArch: box art, screenshot or title screen per console, downloaded or imported from RetroArch
- Autosave on exit and while playing, with an Autosave slot next to 4 save slots
- Fast-forward up to 8x, with lower or muted audio and an on-screen button per console
- Display filters: CRT, LCD, Scale2x, xBR, SABR, MMPX, CRT (zfast) and HD mode
- One up-to-date core per system: the most popular are built in, the others download when first needed
- Gamepad support, local multiplayer, customizable touch controls, tilt input, quick save/load and Google Drive save sync

Supported systems:
- Nintendo: NES, SNES, Nintendo 64, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS, Nintendo 3DS (64-bit ARM devices), Virtual Boy, Pokémon Mini
- Sega: Master System, Game Gear, Genesis / Mega Drive, Sega CD, 32X, SG-1000, Dreamcast
- Sony: PlayStation, PlayStation Portable
- SNK: Neo Geo CD, Neo Geo Pocket, Neo Geo Pocket Color
- Atari: 2600, 5200, 7800, Lynx
- Arcade (FinalBurn Neo, MAME 2003-Plus)
- Others: PC Engine / TurboGrafx-16, WonderSwan, WonderSwan Color, ColecoVision, Intellivision, Vectrex, DOS

Not every device can run every system: Nintendo DS, 3DS, PSP and Dreamcast need a fast phone.
