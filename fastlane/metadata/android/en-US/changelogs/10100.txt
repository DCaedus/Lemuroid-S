First release of Lemuroid-S, a fork of Lemuroid.
- One Lemuroid-S folder for ROMs, BIOS, saves, states and covers
- RetroArch-compatible saves and states, plus Import Saves
- Autosave slot, fast-forward and turbo buttons
- Covers like RetroArch, new display filters
- Up-to-date cores and 10 new systems, including Dreamcast, 3DS and Neo Geo CD
- Core settings in Settings, new themes and setup screen
